FAQ

Good questions to ask

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, which is why every project starts with a free discovery call and a written, fixed-price quote. Landing pages and small tools can be a few weeks of work; full platforms are scoped in phases so you can launch sooner and spread the investment.

How long until I have something I can use?

Most MVPs reach a usable first version in 4–10 weeks. You'll see a working demo every week along the way, not just at the end.

What happens after launch?

You're not on your own. Launch is the start of our working relationship, not the end of it. You keep a dedicated point of contact (me) for bug fixes, updates, questions and new features, and the person supporting your app is the person who built it.

Do I own the code?

Yes, 100%. The code lives in your repository and your hosting accounts from day one, and it's documented so any developer can work on it later.

I'm not technical. Is that a problem?

Not at all. I'll translate the technical decisions into plain language and trade-offs, so you can make confident calls about your product.

Can you take over or improve an existing app?

Absolutely. I can audit what you have, fix what's broken, and either extend it or plan a migration, including moving off no-code platforms like Bubble.
